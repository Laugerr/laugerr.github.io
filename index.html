About Me

👋 Hi, I’m Salah — a Cybersecurity Management MSc student with a solid background in Software Engineering 💻. I care about building things that actually work — secure, purposeful, and built to last 🔒.

🛡️ My focus sits at the intersection of Application Security and software development 📊. I study how systems are attacked, and I apply that thinking while building them ⚙️ — because the best time to think about security is before something goes wrong 🌐.

💡 I have a strong bias toward shipping. I believe the best way to learn is to build something real, get it live, and improve from there. I move fast without cutting corners on what matters — clarity, structure, and making sure the thing actually does what it’s supposed to do 🎯.

🌿 Outside the screen, I recharge through football ⚽, fishing 🎣, and hiking ⛰️ — they keep me grounded and sharpen the focus I bring back to the work 💪. My goal is to grow into an Application Security Engineer who builds systems that are hard to break and easy to trust 📈.
